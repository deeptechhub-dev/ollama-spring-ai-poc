Spring AI provides abstractions for integrating artificial intelligence
models into Spring applications.

Spring AI supports ChatModel implementations for interacting with
large language models.

EmbeddingModel converts text into numerical vector representations
called embeddings.

VectorStore stores document embeddings and performs semantic
similarity searches.

ChatMemory helps applications maintain conversation context by
storing and retrieving previous messages.

Retrieval Augmented Generation, also known as RAG, retrieves relevant
external knowledge and provides it to a language model as context.
